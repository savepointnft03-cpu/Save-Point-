[ WHAT IS SAVE POINT? ]

NOT JUST
A FILE.

Save Point is a planned 3,333-piece NFT art collection that reimagines the 3.5-inch floppy disk as a digital artifact. Every design keeps a familiar shape while exploring new combinations of color, texture, labels and symbols.

We plan to launch on Robinhood Chain with a free mint (gas fees apply), subject to technical readiness and deployment.

[ QUESTIONS / ANSWERS ]

FAQ.

Is minting live?

No. This is a pre-launch website. There is no deployed NFT contract connected to this site.

How many NFTs will exist?

The planned maximum collection size is 3,333 unique NFTs. Final art and metadata still need to be produced and reviewed.

How much does minting cost?

The planned mint price is 0 ETH, with blockchain gas fees paid by the user.

How many can I mint?

The planned wallet limit is 3 NFTs, to be enforced by the future smart contract.

Which blockchain?

Robinhood Chain is the selected target. Exact network parameters and contract deployment will be confirmed before live minting.

Are these the final NFT images?

No. The collection page contains 15 visual concepts extracted from the approved concept sheet. They are previews, not the completed 3,333 artworks.

Does connecting a wallet cost money?

No. Connecting an injected EVM wallet here requests account access only. It does not request a signature, approval or transaction.
